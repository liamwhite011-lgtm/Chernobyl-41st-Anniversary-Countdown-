How the clock works

The browser first synchronizes against an internet time service for America/Toronto, then uses a monotonic browser timer between syncs so the display stays smooth. Historical event times are converted from the plant's 1986 Moscow daylight time (UTC+4) to Toronto's 2027 daylight time (UTC−4). This makes 01:23:40 at Chornobyl land at 17:23:40 (5:23:40 p.m.) Toronto time on April 25, 2027.

Historical timing follows the Chornobyl Nuclear Power Plant's published chronology for the test start (01:23:04), AZ-5 (01:23:39), rapid power increase (01:23:43), and explosions. Some later response times are intentionally approximate because the historical record gives broader time windows rather than a precise second.
